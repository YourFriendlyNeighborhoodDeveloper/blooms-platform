# The Blooms platform: an overview

This folder explains, in plain language, what the platform does, what the business needs to provide,
and how it would be set up for real. It was prepared by Abraxas Technologies.

> **Status.** The platform is a working demonstration. Everything in it is fictional: the products,
> customers, staff, orders and weddings. Payments are simulated and no money moves. Messages are
> captured, not sent. It is not an authorized, operating Blooms storefront, and it will not become one
> without the owner's agreement and approval.

## What customers can do, at any hour

- **Shop** flowers and boutique pieces, choose sizes, colors and card messages, and pay online.
- **Choose pickup or delivery**, from times the shop can actually meet. The system knows opening hours, how long each piece takes, how busy the day is, delivery areas, and when the driver is working.
- **Track an order** on a private page that updates as the team works: preparing, ready, out for delivery, done. Each item has its own progress.
- **Plan a wedding or event** in seven steps, at 11:30 p.m. if they like: date, venue, colors, inspiration photos, flowers and rentals. They see an honest estimate and whether the date looks open, send the plan, and book a consultation from the times the team has free.
- **Review and accept a quote, and pay a deposit** online. Their project page then shows progress and what is still owed.

## What the team can do

- See everything that needs attention in one place, the moment it arrives.
- Accept, decline or question orders, move each item along, assign staff, reschedule within the rules, refund, and print packing slips.
- Work wedding inquiries from first message to confirmed project, with quotes, deposits, tasks and rentals.
- Keep stock, recipes, suppliers and purchase orders in step, with low-stock alerts.
- See one shared calendar of orders, deliveries, consultations, events, setup, teardown and days off.
- Change prices, products, hours, delivery rules, capacity and policies without a developer.

## What the system protects

- It never takes an order the shop cannot fulfil: no overselling, no impossible delivery times, no double-booked crews or rentals.
- Nothing is marked paid until the payment provider confirms it.
- Important rules wait for the owner's approval. Until then, orders go to a person to review.
- Each person on the team sees only what their role allows. Every sensitive change is recorded.
