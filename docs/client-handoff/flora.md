# Flora, your AI concierge

Flora answers questions on the website and, if you want, on a phone line, any time of day. She is an
automated assistant, and she says so at the start of every chat and call.

## What she does for you

- **Answers the common questions** from your settings, your shop and the answers you write for her.
- **Plans weddings with customers.** She asks about the date, the venue, guests, colors and
  budget. She checks the date against your real calendar and saves the plan as she goes. Then she
  sends the customer a private link to review and send it. The plan arrives in your Pipeline like
  any other.
- **Helps with orders, safely.** She emails a code to the address on the order before saying
  anything about it, and can send the tracking link.
- **Passes things to a person** when someone asks for one, is unhappy, has something urgent, or
  wants something only you can decide: a refund, a change, an exception. You choose who hears about
  each kind of request. If an urgent one is not acknowledged in time, it goes to the owner.

## What she will not do

She will not make up prices, dates or policies, reserve anything, take payments, change or refund an
order, or pretend to be you or anyone on the team.

## Your controls (Dashboard → Settings → Flora)

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Answer website chats / Answer the phone line | Switch each one on or off |
| Works with | **Demonstration sandbox** (fictional data, nothing reaches your pipeline) or **the real business** |
| Monthly spending limit | Flora stops for the month when the estimated spend reaches it. Zero keeps her off |
| Greetings | What she says first. Keep the line saying she is automated |
| Call and chat limits | Call length, calls per number per day, messages per chat |
| Keep transcripts / delete after | How long the words of conversations are kept |
| Who hears about requests | Which roles get each kind of request, and who gets escalations |

## Teaching her (Dashboard → Flora → Knowledge)

Write an answer the way you would say it, then tick **Published**. Flora uses only published
answers. If she does not know something, she says so and offers to have someone get back to the
customer.

## Watching her work (Dashboard → Flora)

- **Overview:** this month's estimated spend against your limit, conversations, and open requests.
- **Conversations:** every chat and call, with what was said, each lookup she made, and anything
  the system refused.
- **Messages:** requests Flora passed on, most urgent first, with an **Acknowledge** button.

## What it costs

You pay the AI provider and Twilio directly, as you use them. A typical website chat costs a few
cents, and a five-minute phone call roughly 40 to 60 cents including phone time. See
[../costs-and-services.md](../costs-and-services.md). Flora never buys credits on her own.

## Before real customers

- Your own OpenAI account and key, with a budget.
- A phone number for Flora. Texting the planning link also needs carrier registration (A2P 10DLC)
  in the business's name.
- Your hours, policies and knowledge answers filled in.
- Both channels switched to **the real business** only when you are ready.
