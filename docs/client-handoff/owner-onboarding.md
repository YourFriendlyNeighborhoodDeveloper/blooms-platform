# What the business provides

Everything here is the business's to decide. Nothing has been guessed from public listings. Once a
real account is set up, the team dashboard has a page called **Set up your shop** that walks through
the same list, in this order, and ticks each step off.

Until a rule is entered and approved, the system plays safe. It offers fewer times rather than too
many, and sends orders to a person rather than accepting them automatically.

## 1. The business

- [ ] Official business name, as customers and receipts should show it
- [ ] Phone, public email, street address, and the Facebook page
- [ ] A website domain the business owns, or the plan to buy one
- [ ] Opening hours for each day, and closed days and holidays

## 2. What you sell

- [ ] Departments, such as Floral, Boutique, and Weddings & Events
- [ ] Each product: name, description, photos, price, sale price if any, and sizes or variants
- [ ] Stock on hand for anything counted, and an alert level for running low
- [ ] Preparation time for each floral product, and how much of a day it uses
- [ ] How each product can be received: pickup, local delivery, shipping
- [ ] Any seasonal products, and their season
- [ ] Recipes, if you want stock of stems and supplies to go down automatically

Products can be added one at a time, or many at once from a spreadsheet. See
[product-import-guide.md](product-import-guide.md).

## 3. Weddings and events

- [ ] Services and packages, with a fixed price, an estimate range, or "custom quote" for each
- [ ] Rental items, how many you own, and the cleaning time each needs after return
- [ ] Event crews: how many can work at the same time, and how many people make a crew
- [ ] Most events in one day, minimum notice, setup, teardown and travel times
- [ ] Deposit rules: how much, when the balance is due, and whether deposits are refundable
- [ ] Cancellation policy
- [ ] Consultation length, and which team members take consultations

## 4. The team

- [ ] Each person's name and email, and what they do
- [ ] Their usual weekly schedule, and known days off
- [ ] What each person should be allowed to see and do. See [staff-setup.md](staff-setup.md).

## 5. Delivery and pickup

- [ ] Delivery areas by ZIP code, the fee for each, and any minimum order
- [ ] Delivery and pickup times for each day, and the order cutoff for each
- [ ] The most deliveries per window, and who drives
- [ ] Whether same-day orders are offered, and the cutoff time
- [ ] Whether to ship, where to, and at what price

See [delivery-rules.md](delivery-rules.md).

## 6. Money

- [ ] A Stripe account in the business's name. See [payments-setup.md](payments-setup.md).
- [ ] Sales tax rates, and what is taxable, confirmed by your accountant
- [ ] Refund policy, and how much non-owners may refund

## 7. Messages to customers

- [ ] The sending address, on your own domain, such as orders@ your domain
- [ ] Which messages to send, and their wording. Each can be edited, previewed and switched off.
- [ ] Whether to use text messages at all. See [notifications-setup.md](notifications-setup.md).

## 8. Legal

- [ ] Privacy policy and terms of sale, written or reviewed by your lawyer

## 9. Approvals

The owner approves these in the dashboard, and only an owner can:

- [ ] Ordering rules
- [ ] Delivery rules
- [ ] Sales tax
- [ ] Refunds
- [ ] Going live, which removes the "concept" notices and lets search engines list the site
