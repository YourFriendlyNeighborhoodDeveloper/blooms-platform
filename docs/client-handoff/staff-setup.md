# Your team: people, roles and schedules

All of this is under **Team** in the dashboard.

## Inviting someone

Press **Invite someone**, enter their name and email, tick what they do, and choose their role.
They get an email with a link to set their own password. Nobody else ever sees it.

**What they do** decides where the system can count on them:

- **Prepares orders** counts them toward the day's production capacity.
- **Drives deliveries** lets delivery be offered on days they work.
- **Works events** counts them toward event crews.
- **Takes consultations** offers their free times to customers booking a consultation.

## Roles: what each person can see and do

| Role | Can | Cannot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including approving business rules, editing roles, and erasing customer data | |
| Administrator | Run daily operations, including refunds and settings | Approve business rules, change team roles, or erase customer data |
| Sales & consultations | Work inquiries and consultations, send quotes, see order totals, manage customers | Change settings, issue refunds |
| Florist & production | See and progress orders, manage stock | See prices, payments or customer financial history |
| Event coordinator | Run confirmed events, rentals and crews, see deliveries and the team | See payments, change settings |
| Delivery | See their deliveries and record progress | See prices or customer history |
| Finance (read only) | See financial records and reports, and export them | Change anything |

An owner can change exactly what any role allows, under **Team → Edit what they can do**. The owner
role always keeps everything.

## Schedules

Under each person, **Schedule** sets their usual week and days off. These matter:

- Delivery is offered only on days a driver works.
- Consultations are offered only when someone who takes them is working.
- Event capacity counts the people available to staff crews that day.

Add vacations and sick days as they happen. The calendar shows them, and the system stops offering
work that depends on that person.

## Event crews

Under **Team → Event crews**, add each crew that can work an event's setup or teardown. A crew can be
at one event at a time. Retire a crew when it is no longer available.

## When someone leaves

Press **Deactivate**. They are signed out everywhere immediately, and can no longer sign in. Their
past work stays in the records.

## Good practice

- Give each person only the role they need.
- Everyone signs in with their own account. Never share passwords.
- Each person can see where they are signed in, and sign out other devices, under **Your account**.
