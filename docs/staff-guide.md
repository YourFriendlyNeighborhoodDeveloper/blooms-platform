# Staff guide

A short guide for everyone on the team. Sign in at `/admin` with the invitation link you were sent.
You only see the parts of the dashboard your role needs.

## Every day

1. Open **Overview** to see what needs doing today.
2. Check **Tasks**. Tick **Only mine** to see what is assigned to you.
3. Check the **Calendar** for today, with your name chosen in the team filter.

## Florists and production

- Open an order from **Orders** or from your task.
- Press **Start preparing** when you begin, and **Mark ready** when it is done. The customer is told automatically, so there is no need to call them.
- If something is short, press **Short on stock**. The shop is alerted, and the customer sees a delay notice rather than silence.
- If a recipe ingredient has run out, **Use a substitute** offers only the swaps the owner has approved.
- Card messages and options appear under each item. Print the **Packing slip** to keep at the bench.

## Drivers

- **Deliveries** shows the day's runs. You see the ones assigned to you.
- Press **Out for delivery** when you leave, and **Delivered** when it is handed over. If proof of delivery is required, add a short note, such as who received it or where it was left.
- If you cannot deliver, press **Failed** and say why. The customer is told, and the shop arranges another try.
- The customer sees these steps. There is no live GPS tracking, so please update the status as you go.

## Event crews and coordinators

- The **Calendar** shows each event's setup and teardown times.
- On a project, the **Tasks** card lists what is due and when. Tick tasks off as you go.
- **Rentals** shows what is going out and coming back. Note any damage when items return. Items are not available again until their cleaning time has passed.

## Consultations

- Your consultations appear on the calendar, and customers book only the times you are scheduled to work.
- After a consultation, mark it **Complete** and add notes to the inquiry.

## Good habits

- Update statuses as they happen. Customers, the calendar and reports all rely on them.
- Internal notes are never shown to customers. Customer messages are.
- Sign out on shared computers. Sessions on the team dashboard end after 12 hours without use.
- If something looks wrong, tell the owner rather than working around it. Every change is recorded.
